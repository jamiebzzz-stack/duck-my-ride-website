Privacy

A little help for the road

Let’s get you rolling.

Duck My Ride is currently in development. These instructions cover the current app; a public download is not available yet.

How do I save a duck?

Open My Ducks, choose Add a duck, and add a photo, name, and story. A QR code is not required.

How do I send a duck card?

Choose Send a duck card. Pick one of the 58 named characters, choose a greeting, and optionally add a nickname. Preview it, then use Share this card on your device to choose the recipient in your messaging app. Sharing does not transfer ownership or confirm delivery.

Where are Settings?

Tap the gear in the app header. You can save a default card nickname and choose whether Home shows trail tips. You can also read the in-app help and privacy information.

Does it track my duck automatically?

No. Journeys stores the towns and memories you record. Live maps, automatic miles, friend connections, and QR-linked physical ducks are future features.

How do I report a problem during testing?

Use the contact channel that provided your test build. Include the version shown in Settings, device model, what you tapped, and a screenshot. Do not include private collection details. Avoid deleting the app to troubleshoot because your stories are stored locally.

A verified public support contact will be added before public release.
