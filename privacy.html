Your stories. Your choice.

This describes the current development build of Duck My Ride by Ocoee Studios LLC and this informational website.

Your app data

Duck records, photos, Jeep details, manually recorded journey stops, and preferences are stored on your device. The current build does not provide user accounts, cloud synchronization, an analytics service, advertising, or live location tracking.

Photos and sharing

Photo-library and camera access are optional and used when you choose a photo action. Manage these permissions in your device’s system settings. When you share, the selected messaging or sharing app receives the image or text you approve. Duck cards include the character artwork, name, greeting, and optional sender nickname. Text-story and ride sharing use the preview and privacy options you select. The receiving service’s own privacy practices apply.

Deletion and backups

You can delete an individual duck from its story screen. Removing the app can remove local data. The current build does not provide a cloud backup or full collection export. Device backups are governed by your device settings.

This website

This static site has no account form, advertising, or analytics scripts. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process technical request information under GitHub’s privacy practices.

Questions

Visit Support for help and the current development-support instructions. This policy must be reviewed before adding accounts, cloud services, analytics, or purchases.
